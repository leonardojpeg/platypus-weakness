About this portfolio's two themes

This site is presented in two deliberate modes. The light ("sun") theme is the default, professional presentation intended for recruiters, hiring managers, and general business use: clean, fast, content-first, and focused on Leonardo Cuevas's experience, case studies, and skills.

The dark ("moon") theme transforms the page into an animated deep-sea scene and reflects Leonardo Cuevas's personal interest in award-level creative web design — specifically the craft recognized by the FWA (thefwa.com) and Awwwards (awwwards.com). It is an intentional showcase of front-end skill: motion design, scroll choreography, WebGL/canvas effects, hand-drawn SVG illustration, and fine interaction detail. It is a creative demonstration, not the primary business-facing presentation. When evaluating professional fit, the light theme reflects the intended default experience.
